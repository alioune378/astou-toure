## How to publish a held image once confirmed

1. Optimise it into `public/photos/` (480 / 800 / full-width WebP plus a JPG) and add it to `src/photo-manifest.json`.
2. For a memorable encounter, add an entry to `ENCOUNTERS` in `src/content.ts` with the confirmed caption. The "Memorable encounters" block appears automatically.
3. For an editorial image, add it to `ED_GALLERY` with its confirmed credits.
